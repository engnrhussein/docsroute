2. Structure & Nature of Legal Network

ATTORNEY ADVERTISING & ROUTING NOTICE: RouteDefense Group operates as a legal technology platform and lawyer network that facilitates initial intake, jurisdictional analysis, and case transmission between motorists and independent licensed defense attorneys. RouteDefense itself is not a law firm and does not provide legal representation directly.

Independent attorneys participating in the RouteDefense Network are vetted, maintain active licensing in their respective state jurisdictions, and adhere to applicable state ethics and professional responsibility rules.

3. Attorney-Client Relationship Formation

Transmitting your citation details, narrative, or uploaded ticket documentation through our website does NOT automatically establish an attorney-client relationship. An attorney-client relationship is formally established ONLY when:

  • The assigned defense attorney conducts an ethical conflict check and confirms availability in your county court venue.
  • Both you and the assigned attorney execute a formal engagement agreement detailing scope of representation.
  • Agreed legal fees or retainers are settled in accordance with applicable state trust account (IOLTA) standards.
